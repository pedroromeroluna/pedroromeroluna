I build products where the math has to be right: promotions, lending, billing, scoring. Thirteen
years shipping digital products, six of them leading squads. At **Mercado Libre** I ran Marketplace
promotions across six LATAM markets; at **Galicia Bank**, a lending product on top of an ML
scoring engine.

Two more are my own. **Product Hub** is the first Product Management school in Spanish for LATAM,
where I'm a partner and Academic Director. **Prego Tech** is an agent platform for construction
firms, starting with the estimate: the bid that used to take days comes back in minutes. I also
teach Business Validation on the UX Research diploma at **Universidad de Belgrano**.

In 2026 I rebuilt the way I work around coding agents, and started publishing the parts I kept
using.
